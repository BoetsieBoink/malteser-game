# The Malteser Game
In "The Malteser Game", you are a prisoner at a high-security prison. You are given a pistol, and in front of you is a silhoutte... The question is: Is it a Malteser, or a fellow inmate? You cannot tell! The lighting is dim, and everybody's shaved bald. **There's only one way to find out the answer...**

A project I made cuz I was bored... Based off of an Idea me and my friends had with a group project where we had to design a (fictional) prison, we came up with "The Malteser Game". It's been a year, and now anyone can enjoy the endless fun of... gambling with people's lives... yoo-hoo..?
