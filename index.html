THE MALTESER GAME

How does it work?

In "The Malteser Game", you are a prisoner at a high-security prison. You are given a pistol, and in front of you is a silhoutte...
The question is: Is it a Malteser, or a fellow inmate? You cannot tell! The lighting is dim, and everybody's shaved bald.
There's only one way to find out the answer...
